What it does

Blocks strangers

Android hands the call to the app before the phone rings. The number is not in your contacts — the call is rejected.

Keeps a journal

Number, time and the reason for every decision: “not in contacts”, “number in contacts”, “scheduled pause”.

Flexible pauses

An hour for a courier, a weekday schedule from 9 to 18, or a one-time pass for the next call.

Allow list

Clinics, deliveries and other important numbers always get through, even when the filter is on.

Works in any country

The country comes from the SIM or the network and numbers are compared in international format — a contact from Berlin will not be blocked.

Installation

  1. Download the APK on your phone and open it. Allow installing from unknown sources for your file manager.
  2. In the app, grant contacts access and assign the “Call screening” role — pick CallGuard in the system dialog, not Phone.
  3. Use “Check for updates” in the About screen, or just call yourself from an unknown number.

Only one app can hold the call screening role. Assigning it to CallGuard turns off the built-in Google caller ID; you can switch back in the same “Caller ID & spam app” setting.

Updates

The app checks the latest GitHub release, downloads the APK and hands it to the system installer. Updates install over the current version — the journal and allow list are kept. If you prefer, download the new APK manually and install it the same way.

The update check is the only network action. The request goes to api.github.com only; no call, contact or journal data is ever sent.

Compatibility

Android 10 and newer

Everything works: screening before the first ring, journal, schedule, in-app updates.

Android 7–9 — no

The system only lets the default dialer screen calls. Replacing your phone app entirely is a different product, so we did not.

iPhone — impossible

iOS gives third-party apps neither the caller number nor the right to reject a call. Detailed explanation.

Honest limitations

Hidden and private numbers are not blocked

Android does not pass such calls to screening apps. To silence those too, turn on unknown caller blocking in the Phone app: Phone → Settings → Blocked numbers → “Unknown”.

The number verification marker is rare in Russia

STIR/SHAKEN is carrier-side verification. The “block without marker” option exists but is off by default: otherwise your own contacts would be blocked too. The journal shows the marker for every call.

Numbers are compared by the last 10 digits

So +7 916 123-45-67, 8 (916) 123-45-67 and 9161234567 are treated as one number. Otherwise different formatting would break matching.
